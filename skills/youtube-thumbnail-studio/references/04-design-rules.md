# Design rules

## Sizes
- 16:9: 1280×720 (minimum), 3840×2160 master; JPG/PNG, ≤ 2 MB, sRGB.
- 9:16 (Shorts/Reels/TikTok): 1080×1920; UI zones: top ~12 % (header), bottom ~25 % (caption), right edge ~15 % (buttons).

## Safe zones 16:9
- Bottom-right ≈ 22 % × 18 % — duration badge.
- Bottom-left — chapters and progress bar.
- Edges ~4 % — device crops; top-right — "watch later" icons.

## Legibility
- **120 px test**: at 120 px wide you can still read the mood (colour, face), the story (layout) and the text. If not, redo.
- Also check 168×94 (sidebar) and 320×180 (phone).
- 3 elements max; each one big; background simpler than the hero.

## Colour
- ≤ 3 colours; more saturated than life; contrast in lightness, not only in hue.
- No warm on warm (red on orange = mud).
- Your dominant colour ≠ your feed neighbours' (pastel niche → go hot/dark, and vice versa).
- Pairs: yellow + black (urgency), red + white (energy), blue + orange (education), green + white (money),
  purple + yellow (premium/creative), white + dark (luxury, minimal). Green = good, red = bad.

## Text
- 3–5 words (fewer is better), readable at 320×180; never on the face; adds to the title.
- Heavy sans for a shout; a serif (Didot/Bodoni) for a premium editorial look — but only big and bold.
- Stroke or shadow for contrast; underline or colour the key word/number.
- Models don't hold exact fonts — composite the final text with the script.

## Background
- A darkened/graded real scene with depth, not a flat fill.
- No extra people, clutter or third-party logos.
