# Thumbnail brief — template

One brief per concept. An empty field means the brief is not ready. A human approves it.

```
# Thumbnail <A/B/C> · "<concept name>" · paired with title "<title>"

1. Click driver and hypothesis: why they will stop scrolling (one sentence).
2. Desire loop: desire · pain · transformation · "if I click — …".
3. Anchor in the video: timecode and quote where the promise pays off (≤ 30 s is ideal).
4. Hero emotion: one word + how it reads (eyes, mouth, brows, hands, pose). Peak frame exists / reshoot needed.
5. Layout: symmetric / thirds / split. 16:9 (or 9:16) map in percent:
   - hero: x…–…%, face …% of height, gaze direction;
   - main object: where, how big;
   - background: which real scene, how dark/blurred;
   - text: zone, lines;
   - keep clear: bottom-right (duration), bottom-left (chapters), edges.
6. Palette: 3 HEX colours with roles (background · hero/object · accent/text) + how we differ from the feed.
7. Light and accents: key, rim, depth; what to remove from the frame.
8. Text: ≤ 3–5 words verbatim, font, weight, colour, stroke/shadow; must not repeat the title.
9. Sources: files/timecodes/photos; what to send or reshoot; 2–3 niche references.
10. Woso: model, tool (generate / edit by reference / background removal / upscale), reference order,
    4-part prompt, number of variants. What the AI must NOT touch.
11. Risks: truth, third-party logos and trademarks, explicitness, age restriction, AI disclosure.
12. Acceptance: readable at 120 px and 168×94; stranger test; stands out in the feed (dark and light theme).
```

## Example (fictional travel vlog: "I quit my job and sailed solo across the Atlantic")
- Driver: awe / scale — a tiny sailboat on a giant storm wave, the creator's face in the foreground.
- Loop: freedom · fear of quitting · did it alone · "I'll see if I'd survive this".
- Anchor: 0:00–0:04 the storm shot, 14:20 the wave from the mast camera.
- Emotion: wide-eyed awe mixed with fear, wet hair, hands on the rail.
- Layout: face x 8–38 %, 38 % of height; boat on the wave right third; text top-left.
- Palette: #0B2A3C deep ocean · #F2F5F7 foam · #FF7A1A life-jacket orange accent. The niche is sunny blue → we go storm-dark.
- Text: "DAY 19", heavy condensed sans, white with dark stroke.
- Woso: `nano-banana-pro`, edit by reference: storm sky and spray, the face stays from the original.
- Risks: no brand logos on sails, real footage only for the wave.
- Rejected by a client in testing: "a frame from the vlog + a caption" — "no intrigue, no emotion, no wow".
